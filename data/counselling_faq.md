# Counselling FAQ (SYNTHETIC)

## About this dataset
All colleges, closing ranks and seat counts in this assistant are synthetic. They were generated by a script for a take-home demo. The data is not official, is not published by any counselling authority, and must not be used for real admission decisions.

## Categories
The data uses five categories. General means the unreserved open category. EWS means Economically Weaker Section. OBC means Other Backward Classes. SC means Scheduled Caste. ST means Scheduled Tribe. Each category has its own closing rank for every college, course, quota and year.

## Quotas
Two quotas appear in the data. The All India quota is open to candidates from every state. The State quota is meant only for candidates whose home state matches the state of the college. Deemed universities in this dataset have the All India quota only.

## Counselling rounds
The synthetic process has three rounds: Round 1, Round 2 and a Mop-up round. Candidates register, fill their choices, receive an allotment, and then accept it or move on to the next round. The closing rank published in this dataset is the last rank admitted after all rounds for that year.

## How to read a closing rank
A closing rank is the rank of the last candidate admitted in that category, quota, course and year. A lower closing rank means the seat is more competitive. A candidate whose rank is numerically below the closing rank was within the admitted range in that year.

## Limits of this assistant
This assistant reports figures that exist in its indexed sources and lists options whose past closing ranks are close to a given rank. It does not guarantee admission, does not forecast future cut-offs, and does not replace a counsellor. Closing ranks change every year.
